Set up in
a few minutes.

One ZIP works on both Mac and Windows. You’ll need desktop Chrome 120 or later and your own Cathay account.

Release notes & downloads
  1. 1

    Download and extract

    Download the ZIP above. On Mac, double-click it. On Windows, right-click and choose Extract All. Keep the extracted award-atlas-extension folder somewhere permanent, such as Documents.

  2. 2

    Add it to Chrome

    Type chrome://extensions into Chrome’s address bar. Turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked, and select the award-atlas-extension folder containing manifest.json.

    Select the extracted folder, not the ZIP. Keep that folder in place after installing.

  3. 3

    Sign in. Open the panel. Explore.

    Pin Award Atlas from Chrome’s puzzle-piece menu. Sign in directly on Cathay Pacific, refresh the tab, and click the extension icon. Choose your route and dates, then Search award seats.

GOOD TO KNOW

Before your first search.

Can I include Oneworld partner flights?

Partner itineraries returned by Cathay are included by default, with airline icons and names on each result. Turn on Exclude Oneworld Partners for Cathay-only results. Availability depends on what Cathay returns for your route and dates; not every partner or itinerary is offered.

How do I update the ZIP version?

ZIP installations update manually. Download the newest ZIP from this page, extract it, and replace the files in the folder Chrome originally loaded. Finish or stop any search, then click Reload inside the Award Atlas card at chrome://extensions. Refresh your Cathay tab. Reloading alone does not download new files. Keep the same installed folder and extension identity to preserve saved searches, preferences and results from v0.4.0 onward. Earlier v0.3.x session results cannot survive the first reload. Settings alerts you when GitHub has an update; Update Now opens its release page.

What if a search pauses or finds no seats?

Keep the Cathay booking tab open and complete any sign-in or verification prompts on Cathay. Follow the panel’s waiting instructions and continue when available. Confirmed no-flight responses count as checked with no matches. Individual data errors can be skipped and retried when offered; skipped or unchecked routes stay unknown. Checked results are kept. Cathay’s website changes or request limits can interrupt searches.

Does this book flights or access my password?

No. Sign in directly on Cathay; Award Atlas does not ask for or read your password and has no permission to read Chrome’s cookie store. It uses your existing Cathay session to search. Confirm availability, miles, taxes, and any booking directly with Cathay.

Where can I get help?

Open a GitHub issue with the extension version and error message. Please leave out passwords, cookies, session identifiers, and member or payment details.
